## Runtime and distribution

- Electron and development tools retain their own licenses; they are specified in `package.json` and `package-lock.json`.
- Font packages include their font licenses; keep them with redistributed font assets.
- `ffmpeg-static` downloads a platform-specific FFmpeg binary. The npm wrapper license does not replace the licensing and source obligations of that binary. Review the exact binary build and retain its notices before distributing installers.
- Codex CLI is an external runtime installed and configured by the user. It is not bundled in this source repository.
